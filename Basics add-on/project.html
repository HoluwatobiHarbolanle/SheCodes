How then have men been able to susutain this way of life, whereby we recognize it as their culture?   • It imitates the actions of men in their dealings within the world.
  • It answers the questions of confusion within men with its long-time documentaries.

literature takes three forms:

Prose is straight on. It's in chapters. It's vocabulary edged. It tells an experience. It's narration.

Poetry is rhythmical rendering it a musical nature. It's in verse form. It's well keen on sounds. It compacts emotions into words, using embellished language. It expresses an experience.

Drama is performance. It imitates actions through dialogue exhibiting conflict. It can be a play or a theatre. It is live.
